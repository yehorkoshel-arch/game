RUN THROUGH KYIV

From central boulevards to river bridges and vibrant residential quarters, discover handcrafted track districts.

Zone 1

Kyiv Streets

Wide avenues, chestnut-lined sidewalks, historic brick facades, and busy pedestrian corridors around Khreshchatyk.

Zone 2

Downtown Kyiv

Grand central plazas, towering glass centers, metro entrance ramps, and rapid turns through European Square.

Zone 3

Residential District

High-rise panel neighborhoods, rooftop freerun connections, courtyard obstacle courses, and vibrant street art walls.

Zone 4

Night Kyiv

Illuminated riverbanks along Dnipro, glowing neon signs, reflective wet asphalt, and the glittering Havanskyi Bridge.

City Roads & Expressways

High-velocity multi-lane bridges, elevated speedways, speeding traffic hazards, and breathtaking panoramas of the ancient Dnipro hills.
